three signs you're having the wrong conversation:
1. your emotions escalate.
2. You walk away skeptical.
3. You're in a deja vu dialogue.

skill for finding the right topic: 
Knowing how to unbundle, choose and simplify the issues involved.

unbundle:
**CPR**
- content
- pattern: "the first time something happens, it's an incident. The second time it might be a coincidence. The third time, it's a pattern."
- Relationship: (go to deeper concerns about trust, competence, or respect)

choose:
finding the right topic to discuss it to choose. What your highest priority is; then choose the issue that stands btw you and that objective.

simplify:
having made your choice, be sure you can state simply what you want to discuss. (not about how to start the conv, but narrow the problem down to a succint statement)
The more words it takes you to describe the topic, the less prepared you are to talk.
Just tell yourself the truth about what you want to say.

be alert of topic changing. content drift.

place a bookmark: clarity is crucial. but so is flexibility. this is not a monologue but a dialogue. other pple have their own wants and needs.
